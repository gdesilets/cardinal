# Corpus location

The synced research corpus is stored in `corpus_2026` to stay within
OneDrive's path-length limit. It contains the complete scoped Discord corpus
for the logical window January 1, 2026 through July 20, 2026.

The folder rename changed location only; files and their contents were not
rewritten. The `j9r` folder is the shortened name for the isolated January 9
revalidation mirror. Historical audit records may retain the original paths
recorded at capture time.
