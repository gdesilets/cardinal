## Scope and evidence standard

This synthesis covers the inclusive requested window **2026-04-20 through 2026-07-20** and uses only the supplied Discord server artifacts. The merge contains **15202 unique messages**, with **39 / 39** primary segments and **15** validated supplemental files. A targeted authenticated-browser audit captured **8 selected permalink contexts / 35 visible messages**, adding **24 messages** not already present in the merged export.

Evidence labels mean:

- **Explicit Discord rule/answer:** direct wording from a captured message or reply; chart-specific warnings still apply.
- **Observed association:** a count or descriptive win share inside the selected, self-reported corpus.
- **Synthesis:** a conservative combination of explicit and observed evidence.
- **Insufficient evidence:** the required denominator or direct answer is absent.
- **Authority tier:** named-mentor direct replies, community direct replies, and adjacent context are kept separate; adjacency is never silently upgraded into an answer.

> Nothing here is a backtest, causal estimate, calibrated probability, or market expectancy.

## Executive answer

The Discord corpus treats rejection blocks as **contextual rejection structures**, not standalone wick patterns. The most defensible workflow is: establish bias or unresolved draw; locate a fresh, meaningful PDA/liquidity/key-open interaction; wait for the required tap and close/trigger; define risk beyond real structure; and target a still-open draw. A technically valid block can still be non-actionable when mitigated, exhausted, off-bias, mistimed, or poorly risk-defined.

## How members identify rejection blocks

The corpus does not provide one universal candle formula. An RB is treated as a meaningful rejection, not every wick. Frequent text components are timeframe selection (1575 messages), volume imbalance/FVG (745), liquidity sweep (398), CE/start boundary (187), and meaningful rejection/wick (49). Direct replies support waiting for the RB close in cited setups, permit a volume-imbalance-based marking in one chart, and reject poor 1m shape before a 15m open. The targeted browser audit adds a mentor reply naming PD arrays, liquidity sweeps, bias, and news as reversal-point context.

**Limit:** Several replies are chart-specific. Candle color, liquidity-sweep necessity, and CE/start selection are not universalized.

## Operational model cards

### Model 1: 10AM key-open rejection model

A time-boxed key-open setup: eligibility depends on interaction with the stated 10AM level, so later generic rejection-block trades are not the same model.

- **Context/identification:** Establish a directional bias or unresolved draw before treating the 10AM line as actionable.; For the strict variant, price must interact with or fully tap the stated 10AM key open.; Require overlapping location/context such as an RB, FVG, order block, or OTE/fibonacci area.; When the direct-limit variant is not explicitly used, wait for the stated RB/CISD trigger to close.
- **Entry/confirmation:** Use the documented direct-key-open limit variant only when its full context is present.; Otherwise enter only after the documented closed RB/CISD confirmation.
- **Invalidation/no-trade:** The selected-corpus rule places the stop beyond the structure/wick that makes the reversal wrong; no universal point distance is supplied.; Do not classify a nearby block as the strict setup when the full 10AM tap has not occurred.; Stop considering new 10AM-model entries at the corpus-stated 11AM cutoff.; Exclude entries described as chop, unsupported by higher-timeframe context, or contrary to bias.; No full tap in the strict variant, the intended draw already delivered, bias conflict, or the stated time cutoff has passed.
- **Risk/stop:** Place risk beyond the actual rejecting structure rather than at an arbitrary point inside a large wick.; Treat lower-timeframe refinement as optional because tight entries were also described as edge-out risk.
- **Target/management:** Target the stated open draw, swing, or opposing liquidity rather than assuming one fixed R multiple.; Management/scaling is trader-specific; preserve explicit partial or breakeven rules when stated.
- **Strict selected-corpus outcome:** 38W/102L; evidence status `documented`. Counts are overlapping, descriptive corpus associations—not expectancy.

### Model 2: Higher-timeframe PDA into lower-timeframe rejection entry

A location-to-trigger sequence that is not tied to one clock time: higher-timeframe PDA/bias provides context and a lower-timeframe RB/CISD supplies execution.

- **Context/identification:** Start from an explicit higher-timeframe bias, draw, or PDA rather than an isolated lower-timeframe signal.; Require price to reject a meaningful PDA/liquidity location; a random wick is not enough.; Use a stated lower-timeframe RB/CISD or displacement event as the execution trigger.; Wait for the trigger close when the source setup defines close confirmation.
- **Entry/confirmation:** Execute at the documented start/CE only when that variant is explicit; otherwise use the stated 30s/1m/5m trigger.; Do not front-run a trigger that the source requires to close.
- **Invalidation/no-trade:** Operational invalidation is beyond the rejecting wick/structure or the trigger structure; the corpus does not establish one universal close-through rule.; Exclude already-mitigated rejection blocks for a fresh entry.; Exclude the trade when the intended draw has already delivered even if the pattern remains technically valid.; Exclude poor/random lower-timeframe blocks, missing confirmation, and bias-conflict entries.; Bias conflict, delivered draw, mitigated location, missing required tap, or unresolved opposing liquidity.
- **Risk/stop:** Keep the stop outside the structure that defines the setup; reject arbitrary inside-wick risk.
- **Target/management:** Use the unresolved draw or opposing liquidity named in the setup as the target.
- **Strict selected-corpus outcome:** 27W/56L; evidence status `documented`. Counts are overlapping, descriptive corpus associations—not expectancy.

### Model 3: Market-maker model with standard-deviation zone and breaker entry

A narrative-and-location model: an MMXM/MMBM/MMSM sequence and standard-deviation area define context, while a breaker/order-block structure defines execution.

- **Context/identification:** Require an explicit market-maker model narrative rather than labeling any reversal MMXM.; Require an explicitly stated standard-deviation reaction/location.; Use the stated breaker or order-block structure for entry confirmation.
- **Entry/confirmation:** Enter from the documented breaker/order-block trigger after the model reaches its stated reversal zone.
- **Invalidation/no-trade:** The breaker/order-block structure used for entry must remain intact under the source's stated stop; no universal tick distance is inferred.; Do not promote a standalone breaker without the model narrative and stated location.; Exclude trades described as forced, against bias, or inside chop.; The expected market-maker sequence fails, location is absent, or the entry is contrary to the stated bias/draw.
- **Risk/stop:** Place the stop beyond the entry structure or explicitly stated invalidation point.
- **Target/management:** Target the liquidity/draw specified by the model; no fixed corpus-wide multiple is assumed.
- **Strict selected-corpus outcome:** 2W/5L; evidence status `documented`. Counts are overlapping, descriptive corpus associations—not expectancy.

### Model 4: Liquidity sweep, displacement, and FVG/IFVG retrace

An event-sequence model rather than an RB-at-level model: liquidity is swept, structural displacement confirms the turn, and the imbalance retrace provides execution.

- **Context/identification:** Require an explicit liquidity sweep or run on a named high/low/liquidity pool.; Require displacement, BOS, or CISD after the sweep; the sweep alone is not the trigger.; Use the documented FVG/IFVG retrace or retest as entry location.
- **Entry/confirmation:** Enter on the documented FVG/IFVG retest after structural confirmation, not merely on the sweep.
- **Invalidation/no-trade:** The stop is outside the sweep/reversal structure or the explicitly stated entry structure; no universal distance is inferred.; Exclude entries taken before the required displacement/structure confirmation closes.; Exclude setups with unresolved opposing-index liquidity when the source treats it as a blocker.; Exclude premature lower-timeframe entries described as front-running the planned retrace.; No displacement/structure confirmation, unresolved opposing liquidity, or failure to retrace the planned location under the stated rules.
- **Risk/stop:** Keep risk beyond the sweep or confirmed entry structure and avoid arbitrary ultra-tight placement.
- **Target/management:** Use the next named opposing liquidity/draw or explicit R target from the source episode.
- **Strict selected-corpus outcome:** 2W/0L; evidence status `provisional_derived`. Counts are overlapping, descriptive corpus associations—not expectancy.
